Privacy Policy

Travel Translator · Last updated 5 September 2026

The short version: this app collects nothing.

There is no account, no sign-up, no server run by us, and no analytics or advertising software in the app. Everything below explains exactly why that is true, so you can check it rather than take our word for it.

Translation

By default, translation happens entirely on your phone using Google's ML Kit on-device models, after you download the language packs once over Wi-Fi. That text never leaves your device.

Settings has an optional switch, "Use the internet when it is available." When you turn this on, the text you type or speak is sent to MyMemory, a third-party translation service, to get a translation back — no name, account, or device identifier is sent with it, only the text itself. This switch is off by default on Android (which has a full on-device engine) and on by default in the browser preview (which has no on-device engine to fall back to). The app always labels each result with where it came from — your phone, the internet, or the built-in phrasebook — so you always know which just happened.

Microphone

If you use voice input, audio is processed by your phone's own speech-recognition service (or, on Android, Google's on-device recogniser) to turn it into text. The app itself does not record, store, or transmit audio anywhere. The microphone permission is optional — the app is fully usable by typing if you decline it or if your phone has no microphone.

Internet permission

The app requests internet access for exactly two things:

  • downloading the offline language packs (a one-time, ~30 MB-per-language download), and
  • the optional online-translation switch described above, only while it is turned on.

Once the language packs are downloaded, the app can be used indefinitely with no connection at all.

The message log

Every translation you make is saved in a log stored only on your own phone, so you can search or export it later as a CSV file. It is never uploaded anywhere. You can delete the entire log at any time from Settings → Privacy → Delete every message.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No crash-reporting service.
  • No accounts, no passwords, nothing to reset.
  • We never sell or share your data, because we never receive it in the first place.

Children

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age — there is nothing for it to collect.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date above.
